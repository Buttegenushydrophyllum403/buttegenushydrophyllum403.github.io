📋 What is TalkPaste?

TalkPaste converts your speech into written text. This application runs entirely on your local machine. It does not send your voice data to the cloud. You keep complete control over your private recordings.

The software uses advanced Whisper technology to understand your speech. It cleans the text and pastes the result into your active document or web browser. This tool helps you write notes, emails, or reports at the speed of speech.

⚙️ System Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 operating system.
  • A working microphone attached to your computer.
  • At least 4GB of available RAM.
  • At least 2GB of free space on your hard drive.

  4. Complete the setup: Follow the on-screen instructions to finish the installation process.

⌨️ How to Use It

Once you open the app, you will see a simple control panel. Click the start button or use the defined hotkey to begin recording your voice. Speak clearly into your microphone regarding your topic. When you finish speaking, click the stop button.

TalkPaste processes your audio instantly. It converts the sounds into words, removes unnecessary background noise, and formats the text. It then places the text directly into the box where you last typed. You can use it in Word, Notepad, or even your web browser.

🛡️ Privacy and Safety

Many voice tools send user audio to remote servers. TalkPaste does the opposite. The app processes all voice data on your hardware. No audio leaves your machine at any point. No company stores your voice or your text.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the microphone does not catch your voice, check your Windows sound settings. Make sure the correct microphone is selected as the default input device. Ensure your microphone has permission to share input with other applications in your Privacy settings.

If the software stops responding, restart your computer and launch the app again. If problems persist, check the GitHub page for known issues.
